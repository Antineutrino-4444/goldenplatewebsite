Terms of service

Last updated November 23, 2026

St. Andrew’s College in Aurora, Ontario operates goldenplate.ca. These terms apply when you use Golden Plate to register a school, record lunches, view results or share materials.

Taking part as a school

Register a school only if you have its permission. Before uploading a roster or recording a plate against someone’s name, confirm that your school authorizes that use and has provided any notices and obtained any permissions it needs. A coach can record a count-only plate by leaving the identity field blank.

School administrators should give access only to people who need it for their role and remove access when that role ends. Student volunteers should record lunches with the guidance of a staff member. If you are unsure whether you can look up or share a record, ask your school administrator first.

Accounts and records

Use your own account and keep passwords and verification codes private. Enter accurate information, respect access controls and use school records only for the activities your school has authorized. Do not use results to target or embarrass a student or try to identify people in anonymized records. Tell your school administrator if you notice an incorrect entry, a lost login or access by someone outside your team.

Sharing school results

Check the school’s guest privacy settings before making a session public. Use the school’s location for its map pin and share only details you have permission to publish. A spreadsheet export can include names and identifiers. Review its columns and intended recipients before sending it.

When you connect Google Drive or OneDrive, choose an account your school approves and check who can open the presentation link. Automatic updates can replace your edits to the connected deck. Make a separate copy to keep those edits. An interrupted connection can leave an older result on screen, so check the lunch date before presenting.

Handouts, slides and other materials

The school resources include materials for assemblies and classroom use. Keep the credits when sharing them. Third-party photographs, videos and the Second Harvest case study remain subject to their owners’ permissions. Check those permissions before reusing material in another publication.

Questions or concerns

For questions about these terms, a concern about use of the website or a request to review something published here, use the Golden Plate contact details.
