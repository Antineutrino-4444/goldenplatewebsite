golden plate

Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

St. Andrew’s College in Aurora, Ontario operates goldenplate.ca. Each participating school chooses how to record its lunches, who can sign in to its portal and which sessions to share with guests.

What schools record

A school can record a plate count on its own or link it to a person. When a school uses a roster or scans a student card, records can include a name, school identifier, grade, house or advisor, lunch date and plate category. Schools can also add notes. These records support lunch totals, school rankings and prize draws. Ask your school which options it uses.

Who can see results

Signed-in users see records according to their school and role. Public dashboard links show lunch totals. Guests entering a school’s portal can also view history and draws for shared sessions. Guest privacy is on by default for new schools. It replaces student names with labels and hides identifiers, staff names and notes in guest views. If a school turns it off, guests can see names and details in shared sessions, including draw results.

The public school directory shows school names, school codes and the number of public sessions. A school with a map pin also shares its location. The community counter adds together clean plate and session counts, including private sessions, and shows the combined totals. A school spreadsheet export can contain individual names and identifiers.

Accounts, email and website activity

To manage school registrations and accounts, Golden Plate uses the school name, account holder’s name, username and contact email supplied. Account signup notices include the name, username, school and signup status and go to the school contact and the website’s designated notification recipients. The website team’s designated recipients can also receive school registration details and notice of new website-update subscriptions, including the subscriber’s email.

Golden Plate uses Brevo to deliver verification, recovery, administrative and subscription emails. Subscribers can manage updates using the link in their emails. A session cookie keeps track of sign-in and guest access. Your browser also saves preferences such as appearance and map settings.

To diagnose errors and trace changes, the application keeps a rolling 30-day activity log of requests. It records the action, time and outcome, and the account or guest identifier and school when available.

Google Slides and PowerPoint

If you create a cloud presentation, Golden Plate sends the school name, lunch titles and dates, and school-level counts, targets and comparisons to Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive. It builds the slides from totals without importing individual roster entries. Lunch titles appear in the deck, so use titles you can share with everyone who has the link.

Google grants access to Drive files created or opened with Golden Plate. Microsoft grants access to Golden Plate’s app folder in your OneDrive. Golden Plate uses this access to create your assembly deck and, if you turn on automatic updates, to refresh the same file after results change. Golden Plate uses Google account authorization and file information for these presentation features. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. You control the deck’s audience through the provider’s sharing settings.

A one-time Google Slides copy uses a temporary token in your browser. For automatic updates, Golden Plate stores an encrypted authorization token, the associated Golden Plate account and school, the cloud file ID and link, the theme and the last update status. This lets the deck update while you are signed out.

To disconnect, open School assembly slides, select the school, format and theme, then choose Stop automatic updates. This removes the stored authorization token. Golden Plate keeps the file ID and link so you can reconnect the deck. To remove the file or change who can view it, use Drive or OneDrive. You can also revoke Golden Plate’s access in your Google account connections.

Maps, video and form protection

Mapbox supplies interactive maps. When you search for an external place, the server sends the search text to its Photon place-search service. Opening Street View sends the selected location to Google Maps. Playing the introduction video connects your browser to YouTube.

Protected forms use Cloudflare Turnstile. Cloudflare processes browser and device signals to check whether a submission comes from a person. Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum

Access, corrections and deletion

You or your parent or guardian can ask your school to see or correct a roster entry or lunch record, or to request its deletion. If your account offers Delete my account, using it removes your login and stored cloud connections. School lunch records and their audit history stay with the school. Deleting a Golden Plate account leaves existing files in Drive or OneDrive.

To ask what Golden Plate holds about you, request a correction or deletion, or raise a concern about a photo on the site or in a handout, email St. Andrew’s College at info@sac.on.ca and mention Golden Plate and your school. A school name is enough to start. Please leave student rosters, card numbers and passwords out of the email.
